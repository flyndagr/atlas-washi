## LinkedIn

What if a Markdown notebook felt more like paper?

I’ve been building Atlas, a native Rust app for macOS with Japanese washi-inspired textures, fountain-pen typography, and a canvas for connecting notes.

The aim is simple: make room for thinking while keeping your notes in ordinary local Markdown files. Atlas supports import/export, wiki links, backlinks, and recoverable Trash.

I’m sharing this early prototype as open source under the MIT license. Feedback and contributions are welcome, especially around writing flow, accessibility, and reliable local-file workflows.
