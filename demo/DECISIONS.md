# Film brief
Audience: people who want a calm local notebook. Outcome: understand that Atlas combines paper-inspired Markdown notes with a linked canvas, and find its source.

Selected: real-product editorial showcase. The three alternatives were live interaction recording (too much small interface detail in 18 seconds), narrated tutorial (too dense), and purely typographic teaser (too little product evidence). Still captures preserve the real interface without suggesting simulated interactions occurred.

Four beats: 0–5s paper and writing; 5–10s notebook context and Markdown; 10–15s connected canvas; 15–18s name and repository. A quiet dissolve marks a change of idea; each screen otherwise holds still for reading. No fake cursor or simulated typing. An original pentatonic mallet motif ties the sequence together. Captions work with sound muted. Each aspect ratio has its own composition.

The demo surround now uses the app’s default Kozo sheet color and procedural pulp/fiber recipe at 0.7 grain. Chosen over flat cream or a photographic stock texture to keep the product identity consistent. The paper remains stationary across dissolves, preserving readable captions.
